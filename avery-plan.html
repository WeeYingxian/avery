Where this plan stands today

A baby born after 1 April 2027 lands in Singapore's new SG Child Support Package — announced at the 2026 National Day Rally, replacing Baby Bonus. That is roughly $70,000 of support to age 17, and about $25,000 of it arrives in the first year. Everything below is costed against that.

The five decisions that move the money

What to do in the next 90 days

Pre-conception window — these are the ones that expire

Check the hospital plan is in force before you conceive

The Integrated Shield Plan is the one that has to already exist. Pregnancy becomes a pre-existing condition, and these plans carry a 10-month waiting period before pregnancy complications are covered — so upgrading once you are pregnant does not help for this pregnancy. The action here is to verify what you already hold, not to buy something new.

The standalone maternity plan is a separate thing, bought during pregnancy. That decision belongs in the second trimester.

Check your employment runway

Government-paid maternity leave needs 3 continuous months of service with your employer before the birth. If either of you is thinking of switching jobs, the timing matters.

Start folic acid now

400µg daily, ideally from 3 months before conception. Also worth doing pre-conception: dental check, rubella/varicella immunity blood test, review of any long-term medication.

Buying notes

Buy the car seat new

A second-hand seat has an unknown crash history and shells degrade with age and heat. This is the one category where Carousell is a false economy. Check ISOFIX anchor points in your own car before choosing, and note that Singapore law requires a child restraint in a private car.

Buy the pump from a local distributor

Grey-market Spectra and Elvie units are common on marketplaces and carry no local warranty or servicing. A motor failure at week three with no warranty is expensive misery. Mothercare, Motherswork and the brands' official Lazada and Shopee stores are the safe routes.

Premium prams hold value

Bugaboo, Stokke, UPPAbaby and Joolz retain roughly 50–70% on Carousell. If you want a premium pram, the real cost over two years is closer to a mid-range one. The reverse is also true — buying one second-hand is low risk.

Delay what you can

Bouncers, high chairs, playmats and most toys are month-four-onwards purchases. Newborn clothes are outgrown in weeks, and you will be given a lot of them. Wash nothing you might return until the baby is here.

Grants & schemes

Your baby will be a Singapore Citizen born after 1 April 2027, which is the cut-off for the new package. You do not get Baby Bonus; you get something larger and flatter.

What changed. At the 2026 National Day Rally the government replaced the Baby Bonus Scheme and the Large Families Scheme with the SG Child Support Package. Payouts start April 2027. Support no longer varies by birth order — a first child now gets the same as a fifth. Old Baby Bonus figures you will still find online ($11,000 cash gift, $3,000 CDA First Step) are obsolete for you.

SG Child Support Package

~$62,000 to age 17

Plus, outside the package: $5,000 MediSave Grant for Newborns credited automatically to the baby's own MediSave account, MediShield Life from birth including newly diagnosed congenital conditions, and Edusave contributions once school starts. That is the ~$70,000 headline.

Two things that must happen at birth registration

Enrol in the package on LifeSG

Done as part of birth registration in the LifeSG app. Registration is a legal requirement within 42 days of birth, but do it in the first week — the Baby Gift and everything else waits on it. If you miss it there you can enrol separately, but it delays the first Baby Gift tranche.

Open the baby's bank account and link PayNow

Cash payouts go to a PayNow-linked account. Sort this out in the third trimester so it is not one more thing to do in week one. The CDA is separate and opens with DBS, OCBC or UOB.

Paid leave — 30 weeks between you

Shared Parental Leave defaults to a 5+5 split and is adjusted only by mutual agreement, so it is a genuine negotiation, not an admin form. Government reimbursement to employers is capped, so check what your own employer tops up. At the 2026 Rally the government also said it will fully reimburse employers for child-related leave up to the cap — useful leverage if HR pushes back.

Tax reliefs and rebates

Total personal income tax relief is capped at $80,000 a year. The Parenthood Tax Rebate is a rebate, not a relief — it comes off tax payable directly and carries forward until used, so it is worth close to its face value.

Announced but not yet detailed

Worth tracking — several land exactly when your child needs them

Cheaper preschool from 2028

Full-day infant care falling to $300/month and full-day childcare to $150/month by 2030, phased from 2028. Your child would start infant care around 2028 — right as this begins. Do not lock into a long private contract without checking where this has landed.

Subsidies regardless of working status details early 2027

Full childcare and infant care subsidies to be extended to all families with Singapore Citizen children. Today the full Basic Subsidy requires the mother to work 56+ hours a month, which penalises a longer career break.

Higher subsidy income ceiling from 2027

Household income ceiling for Additional Subsidy rises from $12,000 to $15,000 a month; per-capita ceiling from $3,000 to $3,400. Re-check your eligibility after the birth even if you do not qualify now.

Extra BTO ballot chance from Feb 2027

First-timer families get one additional ballot chance per Singapore Citizen child aged 18 and below, in every BTO and SBF exercise. It applies to families expecting a child, not only those who already have one.

Merged Childcare Leave scheme date to be confirmed

Childcare Leave and Extended Childcare Leave merge into one scheme covering children aged 12 and below: 8 days a year per parent with one child, 10 days with two, 12 days with three or more.

Employers fully reimbursed announced

Government to fully reimburse employers for maternity, paternity, adoption, shared parental and childcare leave up to the reimbursement cap. This removes the usual employer objection to fathers taking their full entitlement.

Public or private

Both paths, side by side. The money gap is large; the clinical outcome gap is not. What the extra $8,000–$12,000 buys is continuity of doctor, privacy, and control over scheduling.

Ignore advertised maternity packages. Private hospitals advertise $4,088–$4,500 for a normal delivery. That is hospital charges only. Your obstetrician bills separately ($2,507–$4,142 vaginal, $4,142–$5,886 caesarean), the anaesthetist bills for the epidural ($818–$1,090), and daily attendance runs $230–$450 a day. Real bills land at 2–3.2× the advertised package. The figures below are MOH typical-bill data where published, compiled by the source in Sources. Rows marked est are inferred rather than published.

What the extra money actually buys

The middle path worth considering

Private obstetrician, restructured hospital

Book a private OB with admitting rights at KKH or NUH and deliver in a Ward A or B1 room. You keep one doctor through every appointment and at the delivery, but pay restructured-hospital room and facility rates. A KKH Ward A vaginal delivery has a typical bill near $5,780, against $12,597 at Mount Elizabeth Orchard.

What you give up on that path

No government subsidy applies in Ward A or B1 — subsidies exist only for B2 (up to 65%) and C (up to 80%). You also lose the hotel-grade room, the partner stay-in bed and the menu. Room type is not guaranteed and you can be moved.

MediSave, and what it will not do

MediSave Maternity Package limits

  • Pre-delivery expenses (consults, scans, tests): up to $900
  • Delivery procedure: $1,120–$2,770, by delivery type
  • Daily hospital charges: $1,130/day first two days, $400/day thereafter
  • Typical two-day normal delivery: up to $4,280
  • Caesarean, three-day stay: around $5,040

You can use both parents' MediSave. It pays the lower of the actual bill and the limit.

What insurance does and does not pay for

Nothing covers a routine delivery. Not MediShield Life, not an Integrated Shield Plan, not a maternity plan. A normal birth is paid from MediSave and cash.

Serious complications are covered. MediShield Life has covered inpatient treatment for a defined list since April 2019 — ectopic pregnancy, pre-eclampsia and eclampsia, placental abruption, postpartum haemorrhage among them. Integrated Shield Plans cover a subset of that list, and the subset varies a lot by insurer, so check yours specifically.

The catch is timing: Integrated Shield Plans carry a 10-month waiting period on pregnancy complications, so the plan has to be in force well before you conceive.

Two cats and a baby

Manageable, but a few things change before the birth rather than after
Licensing is settled. Both cats are microchipped and licensed, and two is exactly the HDB limit, so nothing is outstanding. Worth knowing for context: the free licensing window closed on 31 August 2026 and fines now run to $5,000, so this is not one to let lapse.

Toxoplasmosis — the Neakasa does most of the work low risk

The parasite comes from cat faeces and only becomes infectious one to five days after being passed. An automatic box clears waste within minutes, so it never sits in the litter long enough to matter. Combined with indoor cats on commercial food that do not hunt, your risk is already low, and most human infection comes from undercooked meat and unwashed vegetables rather than cats.

What is left to hand over: emptying the sealed waste drawer, roughly every four to seven days with two cats, and the monthly deep clean. That is where contact actually happens, so he takes both from the moment you start trying. If she ever has to, disposable gloves and wash hands.

The cot is the real safety issue

A cat looking for a warm spot may settle against a newborn's face, and a newborn cannot turn their head away. This is the one genuine physical risk, and it is easy to design out: keep the nursery door shut when she sleeps, or fit a cot net. Double-sided tape along the cot rail teaches them to stay off. Supervision stays the rule until she is old enough to move away by herself.

Change things early, not at the birth

Move litter trays and feeding stations out of the nursery weeks ahead, so the cats do not connect being displaced with the baby arriving. Set the nursery up early and let them explore it before it becomes off-limits. Shift their feeding and play times now to whatever they will be afterwards. Play baby crying recordings quietly for a few weeks, and leave baby lotion and wipes where they can investigate.

Allergies are not a reason to rehome them

The evidence is mixed and broadly reassuring. Several large studies associate a cat in the first year of life with lower rates of asthma and allergic sensitisation, and a meta-analysis of more than 77,000 children found no association either way. Nothing in the research supports giving them up.

Before the third trimester

A vet check for both: flea and worm treatment up to date, vaccinations current, claws trimmed. Much easier now than with a newborn in the house. It is on the task list under Second trimester.

The hospital days

Two to four days, longer after a caesarean. Line up a neighbour, family member or sitter to feed them, and budget a little for it. Send a blanket home carrying the baby's scent before the baby arrives, so the introduction is not the first time they smell her.
